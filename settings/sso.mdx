**Single sign-on (SSO)** lets users sign in through your identity provider (IdP) instead of a separate Planasonix password. Planasonix supports SAML 2.0 for enterprise IdPs, plus OIDC-based providers (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Auth0, OneLogin, and generic OIDC) where configured.

<Info>
  SSO is available on the **Enterprise** plan. Organization admins configure providers under **Settings → SSO**.
</Info>

## How sign-in works (SP-initiated)

Planasonix uses **service provider (SP)–initiated** SAML for launch:

1. The user starts at the Planasonix sign-in page and chooses SSO for your organization.
2. Planasonix redirects the browser to your IdP with a SAML authentication request.
3. After the user authenticates at the IdP, the IdP posts the assertion back to Planasonix’s **Assertion Consumer Service (ACS)**.
4. Planasonix validates the response and establishes the session.

Users always begin in Planasonix for this flow. Launching Planasonix from an IdP app tile (**IdP-initiated** SSO) is not supported at launch; support is planned for a later release.

## Values your IdP needs

Open **Settings → SSO**, add or edit your SAML provider, and copy:

| Value | Typical IdP label | What it is |
| ----- | ----------------- | ---------- |
| **ACS URL** | Single sign-on URL, Reply URL, Assertion Consumer Service | Where the IdP posts the SAML response. Default shape: `{your Planasonix app URL}/api/auth/saml/acs` |
| **Entity ID** | Audience URI, SP Entity ID, Identifier | The Planasonix audience the assertion must target. Often `{your Planasonix app URL}/saml/{org-slug}` unless you set a custom Entity ID |

Copy these values exactly (including scheme and path). Trailing-slash mismatches are a common cause of login failures.

You can also download **SP metadata** from Planasonix when your IdP accepts a metadata URL or XML upload.

## Configure SAML

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the SAML app in your IdP">
    Register Planasonix as a service provider. Paste the **ACS URL** and **Entity ID** from Planasonix. Prefer signed assertions; Planasonix validates audience, issuer, signatures, and replay protections on the ACS response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exchange IdP metadata">
    In Planasonix, upload IdP **metadata XML**, or paste **SSO URL**, signing **certificate**, and related fields. Assign users or groups in the IdP so they can complete SSO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map attributes">
    Map email (and optional name / group claims) so Planasonix can identify users. Email-format Name ID is the usual choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test from Planasonix">
    Use **Verify** on the provider (when available), then sign in from an private/incognito window starting at the Planasonix login page. Confirm the user lands in the correct organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Certificate rotation

Plan certificate rotation before IdP signing certificates expire. Upload the new certificate (or refreshed metadata), test with a pilot user from the Planasonix login page, then retire the old certificate. Failed rotation surfaces as login errors for all SSO users.

<Info>
  Just-in-time (JIT) provisioning can create users on first successful SSO login when enabled on the provider. Otherwise, pre-provision accounts in Planasonix before cutover.
</Info>
